ABOUT SCHOLARBRIDGE KENYA

Helping Kenyan talent explore a world of opportunity

ScholarBridge Kenya is a starting point for students and professionals looking into international scholarship and study pathways.

OUR PURPOSE

Make the first steps clearer

Finding a scholarship can feel complicated. We help you think through your field, study goals and the information to check as you explore opportunities.

WHO IT IS FOR

Ambitious learners in Kenya

The platform is designed for Kenyan students and professionals considering study or professional development abroad, across a wide range of disciplines.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Always verify with providers

ScholarBridge does not award scholarships or guarantee admission. Confirm eligibility, funding, deadlines and application details on the provider's official website.
