Frequently asked questions

Find quick answers about exploring scholarship opportunities with Mwangaza Scholars.

Who can use Mwangaza Scholars?

The platform is designed for Kenyan students and professionals exploring international scholarship opportunities. Each scholarship has its own eligibility criteria.

Do I need to be in a specific field?

No. Opportunities can cover technology, engineering, health, business, sciences, arts and many other areas.

Does submitting the interest form guarantee a scholarship?

No. Submitting an interest form is not an award or an official scholarship application. Always check the scholarship provider's official requirements, deadlines and application process.

Where do I submit an official scholarship application?

Apply directly through the scholarship provider's official application channel and follow its current instructions.

What should I do before applying?

Review the eligibility rules and deadline, then prepare the documents requested by the provider. Requirements differ from one opportunity to another.
