GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Who can use Mwangaza Scholars?

The platform is designed for Kenyan students and professionals exploring international scholarship opportunities. Each scholarship has its own eligibility criteria.

Do I need to be in a specific field?

No. Opportunities can cover technology, engineering, health, business, sciences, arts and many other areas.

Does submitting this form guarantee a scholarship?

No. Submitting an interest form is not an award or an official scholarship application. Always check the scholarship provider's official requirements, deadlines and application process.
