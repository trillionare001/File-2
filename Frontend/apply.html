MWANGAZA SCHOLARS – APPLICATION PROCESSING FEE NOTICE

Dear Applicant,

Congratulations on completing your application profile. At Mwangaza Scholars, we are committed to helping Kenyan students access international education opportunities and guiding them throughout their application journey.

A KSh 5,000 Application Processing & Facilitation Fee is payable upon submission of your interest form to initiate review, verification and processing procedures.

What the fee covers

The application processing and facilitation fee covers administrative processing and assistance with:

  • Student visa application guidance and processing support.
  • Travel arrangements and flight ticket facilitation.
  • Scholarship application documentation and submission assistance.
  • Guidance throughout the admission and scholarship process.
  • Ongoing support and coordination throughout your application journey.

Important notice – refund policy

  • The KSh 5,000 processing fee is payable upon application.
  • All interest submissions undergo review and selection by Mwangaza Scholars.
  • Applicants who are not selected to proceed will receive a full refund of the KSh 5,000 fee.
  • Refunds for unsuccessful applicants will be processed within 48 hours after official communication of the selection outcome.
  • Successful applicants will receive official communication outlining the subsequent steps in their scholarship application journey.

Thank you for choosing Mwangaza Scholars. We look forward to supporting your journey towards international education opportunities.
